Social Problem of Today

The Problem

People want to feel validated but lose themselves in the process.

People change how they look for other people to like them.

People even change their fashion based on what is popular and trending.

Diet Issues

People even go on healthy diets that end up destroying them

Solutions for the problems of today:

Building Confidence

- Meet with a therapist to express feelings.
- Adopt healthy diets that strengthen the body.
- Try new styles they’ve always wanted.
- Reflect on how much they’ve changed for others.
